Who We Are

Built on Discipline,
Driven by Community.

At ACE Martial Arts & Wellness, we believe that true strength comes from within. Nestled beside the serene Abbigere Lakeside in Bangalore, our dojo is a place where discipline meets warmth — and where every student is welcomed, valued, and guided.

We are not just training fighters. We are nurturing confident human beings. Every class, every belt, every bead of sweat is a step toward a better version of yourself. That is the ACE way.

Our Disciplines

What We Train

Five disciplines. One community. Endless growth.

Taekwondo

The art of kicks and discipline. From white belt to black belt, our structured program builds focus, agility, and confidence at every level.

Aiki-Jujutsu

Self-defense rooted in ancient Japanese martial arts — trained under Sri Ramesh Jodigi, Head of Aiki-Jujutsu India.

Fitness Training

Indoor and outdoor workout sessions designed to build strength, endurance, and physical wellness for all age groups.

Fighting Skills

Practical combat techniques and sparring sessions that sharpen reflexes, build courage, and prepare students for real-world confidence.
