First Time?

What to Expect

Everything you need to know before stepping onto the mat for the first time.

What to Wear

Come in comfortable sportswear — track pants and a sports t-shirt work perfectly. No special uniform is required when starting out.

What to Bring

Just bring a water bottle and your dedication. No equipment or gear is required — we take care of everything else.

The Environment

A collaborative, wholesome space where kids, teenagers, and adults train side by side — learning from each other as much as from the instructors.
